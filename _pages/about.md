I am an Associate Professor at the School of International Economics and Trade, Dongbei University of Finance and Economics.

My research interests include digital economy, artificial intelligence, international trade, global value chains, labor markets, and firm innovation, with an emphasis on economic theory modeling and big-data empirics.

I earned my Ph.D. in Economics from Nankai University in 2023, my M.A. in Economics from the University of International Business and Economics (UIBE) in 2019, and my B.Eng. in Engineering from Shanghai Maritime University in 2016.
