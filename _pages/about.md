# 马欢 (Huan Ma)

I am an Associate Professor at **The School of International Economics and Trade, Dongbei University of Finance and Economics** (东北财经大学国际经济贸易学院).

My research interests include digital economy, artificial intelligence, international trade, global value chains, labor markets, and firm innovation, with an emphasis on economic theory modeling and big-data empirics. Selected works have been reprinted in the Renmin University of China (RUC) Reprinted Materials (Copied Journals), and ranked among the Top 10 Best Chinese Papers in International Investment Studies for 2023.
